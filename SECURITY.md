# Security Policy

## Security Incident Reporting
If you discover a security vulnerability in this project, please report it to us as soon as possible.

You can submit a report by contacting us via email at: security@longsoft.net.

Please include the following information in your report:
- A description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Any additional context that may assist us in addressing the vulnerability

## Security Updates
We will respond to all security vulnerability reports promptly and make every effort to address them in a timely manner. You may not receive a personal response, but we will acknowledge your report and strive to keep you informed of any updates.

## Policy on Disclosure
We ask that you do not disclose the vulnerability publicly until we have had a chance to address it. This helps protect our users and mitigates potential risk to the community.

Thank you for your cooperation!
